Quick start

How to Play

  1. Move with arrow keys, WASD, the on-screen D-pad, or by tapping the map.
  2. Walk into a glowing shrine to answer a safety question. Wrong answers explain why — you can try again.
  3. Each world gives you 3 shields. A wrong tap costs one. If you use all 3, that world restarts (other worlds stay saved).
  4. Earn 7 badges in a world to open the exit portal.
  5. Worlds unlock in order. Finish all 7 for a printable Net Guardian certificate.
  6. Progress auto-saves under your nickname + password. A short backup code can move progress to another computer.
  7. On a question, tap Listen to hear it read aloud. Optional auto-read lives on that same card.
  8. Press Esc to pause. The music button (or M) mutes adventure music — Listen still works unless you stop it.
